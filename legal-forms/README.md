# Legal Forms and Templates

## Purpose

This directory is reserved for blank legal forms, reusable templates, and document structures that may support the PNG Legal Assistance application.

## Planned categories

- Court forms
- Affidavits and statutory declarations
- Applications and complaints
- Agreements and contracts
- Business and commercial documents
- Land and property documents
- Employment documents
- Family and estate documents
- Legal letters and notices
- General legal document templates

## Classification

Every resource must be identified as one of the following:

1. Official prescribed form
2. Officially published blank document
3. Reusable general template
4. AI-generated draft structure

Never present a general template or AI-generated draft as an official prescribed form.

## Verification requirements

Before a form is made available to users, record:

- Resource title and category
- Jurisdiction
- Official source and source URL, where available
- Issuing authority, where known
- Verification status
- Publication or revision date, where known
- Current legal status, if independently verified
- Copyright and redistribution permission
- Any known limitations or required professional review

Do not invent form fields, filing fees, deadlines, procedural requirements, or official approval.

## Privacy and security

- Store blank forms and templates, not completed forms containing personal information.
- Do not commit real users' identification details, signatures, financial details, or confidential case information.
- Future user-completed documents should be handled separately and securely by the application.

## Current status

This directory currently defines the intended structure. Actual forms and templates will be added after source verification and rights checks.
