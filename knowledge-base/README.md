# Legal Knowledge Base

## Purpose

This directory contains legal materials intended to support legal research, information retrieval, and question answering for the PNG Legal Assistance project.

## Suitable materials

- Constitution and legislation of Papua New Guinea
- Court judgments and decisions
- Court rules and procedural materials
- Official legal notices
- Reviewed legal explanations and research notes
- Other verified legal reference materials

## Requirements

1. Prefer authoritative official sources.
2. Record the source, jurisdiction, verification status, and relevant dates.
3. Never invent legislation, section numbers, judgments, quotations, or citations.
4. Do not treat an accessible webpage or file as proof that a law remains in force.
5. Clearly distinguish original legal text from summaries and interpretations.
6. Review legal materials before treating them as verified.
7. Check copyright and redistribution conditions before storing or publishing copies.
8. Do not store confidential case files or unnecessary personal information here.

## Separation from other collections

- `legal-forms/` stores blank forms and reusable document templates.
- `legal-documents/` stores procedural guides, official publications, and other reference documents.
- `data/legal-resources.json` catalogues legal resources across these collections.

## Current status

This directory establishes the intended structure. Materials must be added and reviewed through the project's source-verification process.
