# PNG Constitutional Amendment Register

**Register date:** 2026-10-31  
**Status:** Preliminary research register; not a certified statement of current constitutional law.

## Purpose
Track proposed, passed, certified, gazetted and commenced constitutional alterations separately. A webpage copy alone is not enough to establish that the text is fully consolidated.

## Required amendment record fields
- Unique amendment ID and exact enacted title/number.
- Bill number and parliamentary history, where applicable.
- Date passed and prescribed-majority evidence.
- Certification details.
- National Gazette issue, publication date and page.
- Commencement date and mechanism.
- Sections altered, inserted or repealed.
- Consequential amendments to Organic Laws or other instruments.
- Relevant court decisions interpreting validity or effect.
- Source URLs, source identifiers and checksum.
- Verification reviewer, review date and redistribution permission.

## Review rules
1. Do not infer enactment from a Bill's passage through Parliament.
2. Do not infer commencement without checking the commencement clause and later commencement notices.
3. Distinguish a constitutional directive or CLRC recommendation from an enacted alteration.
4. Preserve each amendment instrument as a separate document version; do not silently overwrite the Constitution.
5. A consolidated Constitution must state its consolidation date and list incorporated amendments.
6. If official records conflict, leave status unknown and refer the issue to a PNG legal professional.
7. Do not mark the Constitution or an amendment in force until evidence is recorded and independently reviewed.
