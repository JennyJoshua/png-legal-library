## Legal categories to cover

Section 9 of the Constitution identifies the Constitution, Organic Laws, Acts of Parliament, Emergency Regulations, provincial laws, subordinate legislative enactments and underlying law as components of PNG law. A list of Acts alone is not a complete legal corpus.

## Source limitations

- DPLGA is an official departmental list relevant to provincial and local government administration. It is a discovery index, not a substitute for each enacted instrument.
- PNGEITI is a useful sectoral discovery index, not the official publisher of legislation.
- Parliament's bills page returned “No results found” during this review. That is a website result, not evidence that no Bills or Acts exist.
- The Office of Legislative Counsel describes responsibilities for legislative advice, vetting, amendments, editing and printing of laws. It should be approached for an authoritative publication pathway and consolidated texts.
- PacLII is useful for locating legislation and judgments, but current legal effect should be checked against primary publication and commencement evidence.

## Required evidence before catalogue approval

For each instrument, capture exact enacted title and number; issuing authority; Bill and parliamentary history; assent/certification; National Gazette issue/date/page; commencement clause and notices; amendment, repeal, savings and transitional provisions; current or historical consolidated text and date; related regulations and delegated instruments; source URL and access date; checksum; redistribution permission; and reviewer/date/evidence.

## Explicit exclusions

Do not mark an instrument `in_force` because it appears on an agency website, sector list or PacLII. Do not infer current version from upload date. Do not copy full texts into the repository until redistribution rights are checked. Leave legal status `status_unknown` until evidence is sufficient and reviewed.
