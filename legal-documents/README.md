# Legal Documents and Reference Materials

## Purpose

This directory is reserved for supporting legal documents that help users understand legal processes, government requirements, and the administration of justice in Papua New Guinea.

## Planned categories

- Court practice directions
- Procedural guides and manuals
- Official legal publications
- Government legal policies
- Official legal notices
- Legal research reports
- Court and government information booklets
- Legal process checklists
- Publicly available legal assistance resources
- Other relevant official reference materials

## Verification requirements

For every resource, record the following where available:

- Title and document category
- Issuing organisation
- Jurisdiction
- Official source URL
- Publication and revision dates
- Verification status
- Legal status, where relevant and independently verified
- Copyright and redistribution permission
- Notes about limitations or required review

## Handling documents

1. Prefer original documents from official sources.
2. Do not assume a document is current simply because it remains online.
3. Do not modify official documents in a way that changes their legal meaning.
4. Clearly label summaries, adaptations, and AI-generated material.
5. Check permission before storing or redistributing copies.
6. If permission is unknown, retain the metadata and source link rather than copying the document.
7. Never store confidential case files or unnecessary personal information.

## Relationship to other collections

- `knowledge-base/` contains materials intended for legal research and question answering.
- `legal-forms/` contains blank forms and reusable templates.
- `data/legal-resources.json` provides the shared resource catalogue.

## Current status

This directory establishes the intended structure. Documents will be added after source verification and rights checks.
